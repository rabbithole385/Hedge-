Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where do your prices come from?

From public market-data providers: Binance for crypto and Yahoo Finance for stocks, forex, metals and commodities. Bonds follow US Treasury ETFs. Prices refresh about once a minute. Stocks and most commodities only move while their exchanges are open.

How do investment plans work?

Each plan follows a basket of real markets. When those markets rise or fall, your plan's value moves by the same percentage (multiplied by the plan's exposure, which is shown upfront). You can withdraw whenever you like and the current value returns to your wallet.

Are profits guaranteed?

No. Every product on NexoraCopy, including trading, copy trading, bots and plans, can lose money as well as make it. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results, so only invest money you can afford to lose.

Why do I need to verify my identity?

Identity verification (KYC) protects your account and your funds. It's required before any withdrawal can be processed.

How do I deposit and withdraw?

Deposit by crypto or bank transfer from your dashboard. Our team reviews each deposit before it's credited. Withdrawals are requested from your dashboard once your account is verified.
