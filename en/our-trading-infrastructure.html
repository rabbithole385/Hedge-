How It Works

What happens behind the scenes, from live prices to your portfolio.

Live price feeds

Crypto prices come from Binance and other markets from Yahoo Finance. Prices refresh about once a minute and are stored as chart history.

Automatic updates

Plans and bots update on a fixed schedule using the real price change since their last update. If a price is missing, the update waits instead of guessing.

Orders at the live price

Buy and sell orders fill at the price shown on your ticket. Markets without a fresh price are paused for trading.

Deposit review

Each deposit is checked by a person on our team before it reaches your wallet.

Identity checks

KYC verification is required before any withdrawal is processed.

Audit log

Every admin action that touches a balance, deposit or verification decision is recorded.

Copy trading results are posted by our expert traders, not executed on an external exchange. Each expert's profile shows whether its figures are calculated from posted trades or set manually.
