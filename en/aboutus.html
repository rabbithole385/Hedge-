Who we are

HedgeInv is a trading and investment platform operated by Hedge Pty Ltd. We built it so that anyone, from first-time investors to experienced traders, can reach sophisticated tools from a single wallet without juggling several accounts.

Our approach is simple: say clearly what the platform does and how it works. Market prices, investment plans and AI bots follow live prices from public data providers, so you can check every move yourself. Copy trading results are posted by our expert traders and disclosed as such.

Why traders choose HedgeInv

Every part of the experience is designed so you can trade, copy and invest with confidence.

One wallet, every product

Fund once and use the same balance for direct trading, copying experts, running AI bots or joining an investment plan.

Transparent returns

Expert win rates, bot risk profiles and plan terms are shown upfront, and the source of every return is disclosed.

Verified accounts

Every client passes an identity (KYC) check before any withdrawal can be processed, protecting your funds from unauthorised access.

Human-reviewed deposits

Every crypto and bank deposit is checked by a member of our team before it reaches your wallet.

Full audit trail

Any admin action that touches a balance, deposit or KYC decision is logged with who made it and when.

Real support

Reach our team through live chat inside your dashboard or by email at support@hedgeinv.com.

Security and trust

Trust is the first thing we trade on, so we would rather show you what actually happens on your account than put up a badge we cannot back up.

Identities are verified before withdrawals, deposits are checked by a person, and every sensitive admin action is written to an audit log. We do not display a regulatory licence on this site unless we can give you a way to verify it directly with the regulator.
