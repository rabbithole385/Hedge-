How bond prices work here

There's no free live feed for individual government bonds, so each NexoraCopy bond market follows the real daily percentage moves of a US Treasury ETF:

  • 2-year: follows SHY (1-3 year Treasuries)
  • 10-year: follows IEF (7-10 year Treasuries)
  • 30-year: follows TLT (20+ year Treasuries)

Why bonds?

Typically calmer

Government bonds usually move less sharply than stocks or crypto.

Interest-rate sensitive

Bond prices generally fall when interest rates rise, and rise when rates fall.

Balance your portfolio

Many investors hold bonds to offset riskier positions.
