Risk & Exposure

Know exactly how much your money can move before you commit it.

Create Free Account

Direct trades: no leverage

When you buy a market yourself, your position is worth exactly what you paid at today's price. You can't lose more than the amount you put in.

Plans and bots: shown exposure

Some plans and bots use exposure above 1x, so they move more than their markets. The exposure is shown on the plan or bot before you invest.

No margin calls

Nexora doesn't lend you money to trade, so there are no margin calls on your account.

Simple ways to manage risk

  • Spread your money across different markets and products
  • Start with smaller amounts while you learn how a plan or bot behaves
  • Watch the 24-hour change and the market's trading hours before you trade
  • Only invest money you could afford to lose
