Investment plans

Each plan follows a basket of real markets, for example gold and silver, leading US indices, or major cryptocurrencies. When those markets move, your plan moves by the same percentage, multiplied by the plan's exposure, which is shown before you invest.

  • See every market a plan follows, with live prices
  • Exposure shown upfront (1x moves with the market)
  • Withdraw any time: the current value returns to your wallet

AI trading bots

Bots follow one clear rule on live prices and re-check their positions on a fixed schedule:

  • Trend following: long markets that are rising, short those that are falling
  • Mean reversion: leans against short-term moves
  • Long only: always holds its markets

You can see each bot's current long, short or flat positions, and its realised results, before you activate it.
