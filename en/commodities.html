Why people trade commodities

Diversification

Commodities can behave differently from equities, especially during periods of market stress.

Inflation awareness

Many investors watch raw-material prices closely when the cost of living is rising.

Supply and demand

Weather, harvests and production decisions can all move prices, often quickly.

A few markets, such as some base metals, have no free live price source. They show as unavailable and can't be traded until a live price is available.
